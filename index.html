Our story

Bringing the village market closer to home

Mana Oori Santha means “Our Village Market.” Our goal is to make traditional Indian ingredients and homemade flavors easier to enjoy for families in the Tampa Bay community.

We focus on familiar products, convenient local ordering and the comforting flavors many of us grew up with.
